Download LensLink

LensLink comes in two halves that talk to each other: a plugin for OBS Studio on your computer, and an app on your iPhone or iPad. Install both — neither does anything on its own.

Part 2

The iPhone / iPad app

Captures the video and listens for OBS. Requires iOS or iPadOS 15 or later. TestFlight is the recommended route: updates arrive on their own, and no computer is involved.

Before you install

Two things trip people up more than anything else.

OBS Studio 32 or newer Check Help → About in OBS. Older versions do not load the plugin.
iTunes on Windows, for USB Apple's device driver ships with iTunes. Wi-Fi works without it; USB does not. Nothing extra is needed on macOS or Linux.
